HOW TO PLAY

1. Move with WASD or arrows. Follow the wide stone path east.

2. Fight enemies with Space. Face them before attacking. Shift dodges attacks.

3. Defeat the guardian and enough enemies to unlock the eastern gate.

4. Walk up to the gate and press E to enter the next realm.

5. Open chests with E, then equip better swords in Inventory.
